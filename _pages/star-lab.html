Building the computing foundations for autonomy that is safe, predictable, and efficient.

STAR Lab works at the intersection of AI, robotics, and cyber-physical systems. We co-design models, runtime systems, sensing and perception pipelines, and computing platforms for mission-critical autonomy, from autonomous vehicles and mobile robots to drones, and we validate our ideas on real hardware.

Research

Three thrusts, one goal: autonomy you can trust

Each thrust works on a different layer of the autonomy stack. Together they make AI-enabled robots safe to deploy where timing, energy, and physics cannot be ignored.

01

Foundation Models for Autonomy

Perception, reasoning, and decision-making for AI-enabled autonomy in autonomous vehicles, robots, and drones.

Why it matters for safety. A capable model only helps a robot if it fits the latency and energy budget of the platform and stays predictable when the scene gets hard. We study how to keep that capability under those constraints.

RT-BEV · RTSS 2024 AyE-Edge · ICCAD 2024
02

Computing Systems for Robotics

Real-time, efficient, and scalable systems support for autonomous intelligence across device, edge, and cloud.

Why it matters for safety. Predictable end-to-end timing, energy-aware middleware, and profiling tools that turn "it usually works" into a guarantee you can put on a robot.

Prophet · RTSS 2022 E2M · SEC 2019 pPerf · profiler
03

Physics Intelligence

Physics-aware and safety-conscious autonomy for robust cyber-physical systems, including autonomous vehicles, off-road platforms, AR/VR, and other emerging applications.

Why it matters for safety. Autonomy that respects the physical world it runs in: vehicle dynamics, energy and thermal limits, and terrain, so that decisions stay safe when the road, the terrain, or the battery is not ideal.

Efficient AMRs · ICRA 2023 Truck fuel rate · TITS 2023

Platforms and testbeds

Ideas are proven on real robots

Our work is measured on hardware, from indoor mobile robots to full-scale vehicles, not only in simulation.

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/research-platforms.jpg" class="st-figure-img" alt="Research platforms: the HydraOne and Donkey indoor robots, the F1TENTH, Clearpath Husky, and AgileX outdoor robots, and the Hydra drive-by-wire vehicle" sizes="(min-width: 930px) 900px, 95vw" zoomable=true caption="Indoor, outdoor and off-road, and full-scale vehicle platforms our members work with." %}
Indoor

HydraOne and Donkey

Mecanum-wheel research robots with 3-D LiDAR, cameras, and NVIDIA Jetson compute, built for fine-grained energy and timing measurements.

Outdoor and off-road

F1TENTH, Husky, AgileX

Racing-scale cars, a Clearpath Husky, and an AgileX rover for high-speed and rough-terrain autonomy.

Ground vehicles

Hydra

A drive-by-wire passenger vehicle with a roof-mounted sensor rig for full-scale autonomous driving research.

Course fleet

Jetson robots and Crazyflies

Four Jetson Orin Nano ground robots with camera and 2-D LiDAR, and three Crazyflie micro-quadcopters, shared with the Mobile Robot Systems course.
