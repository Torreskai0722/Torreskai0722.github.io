I am an assistant professor in Department of Computer Science at Texas Tech University and the director of the Safe and Efficient Autonomy Laboratory (SEAL Lab). Previously, I’m a postdoc research fellow in the Real-Time Computing Laboratory at the University of Michigan, working with Prof. Kang G. Shin. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Wayne State University in May 2023, where I was supervised by Prof. Weisong Shi.

Research Focus

My research focuses on building safe, predictable, and energy-efficient ML systems for safety-critical cyber-physical systems (CPS) — from autonomous driving to robotics and beyond. I develop novel cross-layer systems (model/algorithms, middleware system, operating system, etc.) to achieve these goals. I’m particularly interested in:

  • Foundation Models for Autonomy
  • Computing Systems for Robotics
  • Physics Intelligence
